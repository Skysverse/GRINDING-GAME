# Emberfall 2.0 — The Open Wilds

A fresh start: **a third-person 3D open-world RPG with a class chosen at the beginning**.

- Choose **Warrior, Mage, or Ranger**. Your class determines your story, weapons, and moves for that character.
- Follow an eight-chapter class questline with its own mentor, gathering objectives, enemy hunts, rituals, and defense or escort objectives. There are 24 class quest entries in total.
- Earn four weapons per class, including Dawnbringer, Astral Sovereign, and Evergreen Requiem. Advanced moves unlock through class quests.
- Explore a connected landscape with Hearthglen village, woodland, a lake, Cinderstone Ruins, and the Astral Reach. There are no expedition resets or wave timers.
- Challenge **three world bosses** at their stones, with telegraphed ground attacks, projectile rings, and charges. Boss rematches and ordinary enemy respawns support continued leveling.
- Manual attacks, animated characters, camera orbit/zoom, jumping, dodging, a quest journal, equipment screens, a world map, and personal waypoints.
- Save your class, weapons, location, levels, and quest progress. Death returns you to the village with a 10% gold loss.

## Download

Download **Emberfall-2.0.0-Windows.exe** below. Windows 10/11 x64 and a working **WebGL 2 graphics driver** are required; hardware acceleration is recommended. No installation is needed. The executable is unsigned; allow a few seconds for the portable launcher to unpack.

This is the first playable 3D version, with stylized low-poly graphics and a finite connected world. It does not include multiplayer, building interiors, or voiced dialogue. Native Windows gameplay has not yet been manually verified.

## Controls

**WASD:** move · **Right drag:** orbit camera · **Wheel:** zoom · **Click / J:** attack · **Space:** jump · **Shift:** dodge · **Q / R / F:** class moves · **T:** flask · **E / hold E:** interact · **Tab:** journal · **I:** gear · **M:** map · **Escape:** pause.

Speak to the mentor under the Hearthglen banners to follow your class story and claim rewards.

## Fresh characters, preserved old saves

The new 3D game starts with class selection and uses a **separate save slot**. Your old 2D saves are untouched, and releases 1.0/1.1 remain available. Open Wilds saves can be exported/imported from the Pause menu. Export a backup before replacing a character.

Progression tests and real WebGL browser checks cover the new systems. The downloadable SHA-256 file can be used to verify the executable.
